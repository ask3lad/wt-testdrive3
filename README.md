# Test Drive 3.0 | TD3 | wt-testdrive3

**Ask3lad Test Drive 3.0** lets you set up your own Ground, Naval, Air and Helicopter test drives in War Thunder in a few clicks. Pick any vehicle, choose its loadout, targets, map and weather, press **Apply**, then load the mission from User Missions in game.

[**Download the Latest Release**](https://github.com/ask3lad/wt-testdrive3/releases/latest) · [Discord](https://discord.gg/EQjxvGDjBF) · [Report a Bug](https://discord.gg/vqWFmaJQPJ) · [Suggest a Feature](https://discord.gg/zk6H4quVnd)

> **Alpha:** options marked **Experimental** may not work in game.

### Features

- **Every Vehicle:** ground, naval, planes and helicopters from the current patch
- **Ground:** custom ammo, swappable targets, helicopter and air targets
- **Naval:** swappable targets, carriers, missile destroyers and War Mode
- **Air and Helicopter:** full pylon loadouts, runway and carrier spawns, ground, SPAA, air and naval targets
- **Air Duel:** fight 1 to 128 enemy planes on six maps
- **Experimental:** Weapon Override, Weightless Loadout, Indestructible Airframe, Rapid Fire, Aced Crew and more
- **Share Setup:** save presets and share them with a TD3- code
- **8 Languages:** English, Русский, 简体中文, 日本語, 한국어, Deutsch, Español, Polski
- **Custom Look:** four App Styles or your own picture or video background
- **Auto Updates:** update in one click

### Install

1. Download **TestDrive3-Setup-3.0.0-alpha.X.exe** from the [latest release](https://github.com/ask3lad/wt-testdrive3/releases/latest) and run it.
2. If SmartScreen appears, click **More Info**, then **Run Anyway** (the app isn't code-signed yet).
3. Pick your language, confirm your War Thunder folder and follow the short tour.

Requires Windows 10 or 11 and War Thunder.

### Safe to Use

- Only edits its own mission files and never launches War Thunder or anything else.
- Every Apply makes a backup, and **Undo Last Apply** puts things back.
- **Repair Map and Missions** in Settings reinstalls anything missing.
