# Test Drive 3.0 | TD3 | wt-testdrive3

**Ask3lad Test Drive 3.0** lets you set up your own Ground, Naval, Air and Helicopter test drives in War Thunder in a few clicks. 
Pick any vehicle, choose its loadout, targets, map and weather, press **Apply**, then load the mission from User Missions in game.

## Features

- **Every Vehicle:** Ground, naval, planes and helicopters from the current War Thunder patch, with pictures, filters and favourites filter
- **Ground Test Drive:** Custom ammo loadouts, swappable targets, helicopter and air targets
- **Naval Test Drive:** Swappable targets, carriers, missile destroyers and a War Mode where targets fight back
- **Air and Helicopter Test Drive:** Full pylon loadouts, runway and carrier spawns, and ground, moving, SPAA, air and naval targets
- **Air Duel:** Fight 1 to 128 enemy planes with their own loadout, distance, altitude and speed, on six maps
- **Experimental Options:** Weapon Override, Weightless Loadout, Indestructible Airframe, Zero Gravity, Rapid Fire, Aced Crew and more
- **Presets and Share Setup:** Save your setups and share them with a TD3- code
- **8 Languages:** English, Русский, 简体中文, 日本語, 한국어, Deutsch, Español and Polski
- **Make It Yours:** Four App Styles or your own picture or video as the background
- **Auto Updates:** App tells you when a new version is out and updates in one click

## Install

1. Download **TestDrive3-Setup-3.0.0-alpha.X.exe** from the [latest release](https://github.com/ask3lad/wt-testdrive3/releases/latest) and run it.
2. Windows may show a SmartScreen warning because the app isn't code-signed yet. Click **More Info**, then **Run Anyway**.
3. Pick your language, confirm your War Thunder folder and follow the short tour.

Already installed? The app updates itself. Just click **Update** when the banner shows.

**Requirements:** Windows 10 or 11 and War Thunder.

## Safe to Use

- The app only edits its own mission files and the few game files they need. It never launches War Thunder or anything else.
- Every Apply saves a backup first, and **Undo Last Apply** puts things back.
- **Repair Map and Missions** in Settings reinstalls the Test Drive maps and missions if anything goes missing.
